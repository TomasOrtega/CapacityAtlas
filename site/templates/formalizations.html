Statements here, substantial proofs elsewhere.

Capacity Atlas owns canonical definitions and versioned statements. Long or problem-specific proofs live in dedicated Lean repositories and are linked by immutable commit.

Central repository

  • Shared definitions needed across channel families
  • Canonical problem statements and statement versions
  • Short proofs and API tests, normally at most 25–50 lines
  • No sorry, admit, or unreviewed axioms

External repositories

  • Substantial coding theorems and converses
  • Problem-specific infrastructure and experiments
  • A pinned Capacity Atlas commit or release
  • An immutable proof commit and named declaration
