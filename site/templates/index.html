A registry, not a proof monorepo

Capacity Atlas keeps canonical channel specifications, controlled tags, and versioned, formally stated claims in one reviewable repository. Substantial formally proved developments live in dedicated repositories and link back by immutable commit.

The claim-level organization, immutable proof links, and simple browse interface are inspired by Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures. See the acknowledgements for the exact design influences and citation.
