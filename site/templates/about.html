About Capacity Atlas

A registry of channel-capacity problems and their known bounds.

Reading an entry

Each entry specifies a channel and cites sources for its capacity or known bounds. Mathematical status and Lean proof coverage are recorded separately: an open problem can have formally proved bounds.
