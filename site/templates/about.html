An auditable map of what is known.

Capacity Atlas is a curated registry of operational channel-capacity problems, not a scraped list or a monolithic proof repository.

Principles

Every entry fixes the channel model, operational criterion, normalization, exact result or known bounds, provenance, open bottleneck, and formalization state. Mathematical truth, formal claim coverage, and proof coverage are separate fields.

The YAML records are the source of truth. The website, JSON API, browse facets, and per-problem formalization records are generated views.

Formal Conjectures

The simplified browse interface, controlled metadata, claim-level formalization model, shared ForMathlib layer, and policy of linking long proofs from dedicated repositories were inspired by Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures project. Capacity Atlas is independently implemented and specializes those ideas to information theory. See ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md for the paper citation and detailed attribution.
